About Dr. Shamanie Thompson, D.C.

Hi, I’m Shamanie.

Wife, mother, doctor of chiropractic — and a cancer survivor who learned firsthand that the state of our closest relationships lives in our bodies.

I’ve been given the honor of carrying on my late father’s work — work that repairs relationships, saves marriages, and heals the brokenhearted, physically and emotionally. Alongside the functional medicine clinic I run with my husband in the Twin Cities, I teach Love Intelligence through consulting, workshops and retreats.

The legacy

My father spent 15 years asking one question

Why do some patients heal, while others — with the same diagnosis, the same treatment — don’t?

My father, Dr. Denny Thompson, was a holistic physician and author. Counseling couples in his practice, he kept finding the answer in the same place: the health of their closest relationships. Over fifteen years he refined that discovery into Love Intelligence and the Twelve Ways of Love.

He wrote The Twelve Ways of Love, The Yoga of Relationships and Relationship Medicine. When he passed, the work passed to me — and it is the joy of my life to continue it.

From five languages to twelve ways

Why this is medicine

What happens to your heart happens to your health

Loneliness and relational pain aren’t just feelings — they show up in blood pressure, immunity, and recovery. People without healthy close relationships face a far higher risk of heart disease, cancer and other chronic illness.

That’s why prioritizing and nurturing your relationships is profound medicine — and why this work belongs beside your diet, your movement and your sleep.
