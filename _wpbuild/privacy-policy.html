Privacy Policy

This page was drafted from how the website actually behaves. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Please read it and tell us anything you want changed before launch.

This policy explains what this website collects, why, and what happens to it. It covers myloveintelligence.com and is written to describe exactly what the site does — nothing more.

What we collect

We only collect what you type in:

  • Contact and enquiry forms. Your name, email address, what you are looking for, and your message.
  • Newsletter sign-up. Your email address.
  • The Love Intelligence quiz. Your answers are scored in your own browser. If you ask for the guide, we receive your email address.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site — which pages are read, roughly where visitors are in the world, and what device they use. This is aggregate reporting. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Payments

We do not take payments on this website. Books, workshops and sessions are sold through Square, and one book is sold through Amazon. When you buy, you are handed to that provider and your card details are entered on their system, never ours. We never see or store card numbers.

How your information is used

To reply to you, to send you what you asked for, and — if you signed up for it — to send occasional letters. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Keeping it, and getting rid of it

Enquiries are kept while we are in conversation and for a reasonable period afterwards. You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you at any time, and we will.

Your choices

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold. Write to Shamaniethompson@gmail.com.
